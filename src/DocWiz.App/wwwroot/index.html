Welcome

Pick a chapter on the left to edit it, or create a new one. Views (trainings, handover decks) have their own visual editor; the project manifest is edited as YAML.

Editing: the chapter opens as the real page — click into text and type. Click a table or image to select it; its tools appear in the bar under the banner. Esc steps out. Reading view opens the saved chapter exactly as the handover will render it.

Tables: click a cell to edit it in place. Rows, columns, alignment and column widths live in the selected table's tool bar. Saving writes only what you changed — untouched lines keep their exact bytes.
