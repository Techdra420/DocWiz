# Text, headings and lists

Writing in DocWiz feels like writing in a word processor: click and type. This chapter covers everyday text — headings, emphasis, lists, quotes and alignment — and the typing shortcuts that make it faster.

## Headings and automatic numbers {#headings}

Every chapter starts with its title, the big heading at the top. Below it, use headings to split the chapter into sections. Pick the kind of block in the toolbar's first menu, which shows **Normal text** while you are in a paragraph:

- **Normal text** — an ordinary paragraph.
- **Title (H1)** — the chapter title; there is exactly one per chapter.
- **Heading 2**, **Heading 3**, **Heading 4** — sections, sub-sections and smaller parts.

DocWiz numbers chapters and headings by itself, Word style: chapter 6 has sections 6.1, 6.2 and so on. The numbers come from the order of the chapters, so they update the moment you move a chapter.

::: warning Don't type numbers into headings
A heading such as "2.1 Cleaning" would show its number twice, and the typed number goes wrong the first time chapters are reordered. **Check** reports every heading that starts with a typed number.
:::

### A heading three levels down {#heading-three}

Headings at levels 3 and 4 are numbered too. Keep deep levels for long reference chapters; most chapters read best with only level 2.

## Emphasis {#emphasis}

Select some text and use the toolbar or the keyboard:

{widths="30% 30% 40%"}
| Looks like | Toolbar | Keyboard |
|------------|---------|----------|
| **Bold** | **B** | Ctrl+B |
| *Italic* | *I* | Ctrl+I |
| ~~Strikethrough~~ | S | — |
| `Code` for names, paths and values | `<>` | — |

The **clear formatting** button (the crossed-out T) takes bold, italic, code and links off the selected text.

## Lists {#lists}

The toolbar has three kinds of list. Click the same button again to turn a list back into paragraphs.

- **Bulleted lists** for things in no particular order.
  - Press **Tab** to move an item one level in…
  - …and **Shift+Tab** to move it back out.
- **Numbered lists** for things that happen in order.
- **Checklists** for things to tick off.

1. A numbered list counts by itself.
2. Insert an item in the middle and the numbers below move up.
3. For real procedures, use **Procedure steps** from [Building blocks](dw:building-blocks) — they get big, easy-to-follow numbers.

- [x] A checklist item that is done
- [ ] One still to do — click a box in the editor to tick it

## Quotes {#quotes}

> A quote stands apart from the text around it. Use it for something somebody said, or for the exact wording of a message on screen.

## Typing shortcuts {#typing-shortcuts}

If you know a little Markdown, type it at the start of a line and the editor turns it into the right block as you go:

{widths="35% 65%" caption="Typing shortcuts"}
| Type at the start of a line | You get |
|-----------------------------|---------|
| `# ` to `#### ` | A heading of that level |
| `- ` or `* ` | A bulleted list |
| `1. ` | A numbered list |
| `> ` | A quote |

Inside a line, `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~strikethrough~~` and `` `code` `` turn into formatted text once you type the closing marks.
